## Onboarding

Right after the setup screen, a short full-screen walk-through sets up your workspace:

1. **What you work on:** products, clients or both. This sets the words the app uses ("Products" or "Clients" in
   the sidebar, settings, Home and the palette) and the kind new spaces start as. With both, the sidebar and Home
   group them and the new-space dialog asks which.
2. **Your first products or clients.** The unused sample space (`PAN`) can be renamed, key included, and each space
   can take one line for its [context doc](/docs/context-docs).
3. **Invites** for the people you work with.
4. **Connecting your agent** (see [Connect an agent](/docs/agents)).
5. **Theme** and the keys worth knowing.

Everything after the first step can be skipped. Owners change the work type later in Settings → General; existing
spaces keep their kind. Settings → General → **Run setup again** and the user menu's **Show onboarding** bring the
walk-through back.

## Invite people

In onboarding, or later in **Settings → Members**: enter their email and choose **Create invite link**, then send
the link yourself. Pantry doesn't send email.

- A link works once, for that email address, for 7 days, and the owner can revoke it.
- The sign-up page pre-fills the invited email. If the link has expired, been revoked or already been used, it says
  so.
- People who already have an account on your instance sign in instead. Invited members get a shorter onboarding of
  their own.
